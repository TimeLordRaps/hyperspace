# Apparent Hyperspace × Hypertime grid

[FRAME] This is an integration contract, not an assertion that a literal
two-dimensional (2D) physical grid exists. `×` denotes ordered pairing.
Let `P` be a finite set of stable reality-presentation identifiers. A
Hypertime frame supplies a dimensionless integer display index `h_t(p)`
for each `p ∈ P`. For each occupied index `t`, a Hyperspace slice supplies
a dimensionless integer slot `h_s(p)` for exactly the identifiers with
`h_t(p) = t`. The proposed drawn address is `(h_s(p), h_t(p))`.

| Hypertime index | Hyperspace slot 0 | Hyperspace slot 1 |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | `alpha` | `beta` |
| 1 | `gamma` | `delta` |

These four labels are an illustrative finite projection, not observed
base-realities or a claim that all realities fit four cells. A row is not a
single physical moment; a column is not a physical trajectory. A named
Hypertime advance, Hyperspace observation channel, and actual observation
record are three different relations. The join may retain all three but
must infer none from neighboring cells. Two presentations can share a
Hypertime index and remain distinct branches; an absent observation edge
does not prove unobservability in every possible frame.

[OPEN] Supply stable identity across rows, observational equivalence,
legitimate branching and joining, and a physical or native-form account of
the apparent hyperpheres. [HYPER] Universempiternality may admit such a
view; a finite table neither exhausts nor realizes that whole.
